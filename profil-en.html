About the Office

South Sulawesi Regional Office
of Immigration

The South Sulawesi Regional Office of the Directorate General of Immigration is a vertical agency under the Directorate General of Immigration, Ministry of Immigration and Correctional Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia. This agency operates at the provincial level to execute the duties and functions of the Directorate General of Immigration.

This office serves as the controlling and supervisory body for all immigration activities in South Sulawesi, encompassing the management of Technical Operating Units (UPT), supervision of foreign nationals, and the delivery of optimal immigration services to the public.

As a Type A Regional Office, it oversees 6 Technical Operating Units (UPT), comprising 5 Immigration Offices and 1 Immigration Detention House, covering the entire South Sulawesi region.

Journey

History & Transformation

The South Sulawesi Regional Office traces its roots to the institutional development of Indonesian immigration since the early days of independence, continuously evolving alongside growing international mobility and the need for provincial-level coordination.

Early Independence
Foundation of Indonesian Immigration
Management of international cross-border movement was conducted simply and centralized at several key entry points. The government began establishing immigration units in various regions, including South Sulawesi.
Development Period
Establishment of Regional Offices
The need for provincial-level coordination led to the formation of regional offices, serving as a bridge between the central government and local technical operating units across South Sulawesi.
Ministry of Law & HR Era
Under the Ministry of Law and Human Rights
Immigration functions were placed under the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia, with the Regional Office coordinating all UPT activities across South Sulawesi.
2024
Institutional Transformation
The government established the Ministry of Immigration and Correctional Affairs, responding to evolving organizational needs and the growing complexity of immigration tasks across Indonesia.
2025
Gradual Implementation
The institutional transformation began its gradual implementation, strengthening the Regional Office's role in carrying out the functions of immigration services, supervision, and law enforcement.
2026 — Present
Expansion & New UPT Units
Two new UPT offices began operations: Immigration Office Bone (January 2026) and Immigration Office Bantaeng. The Regional Office now oversees 6 UPTs, providing broader coverage across South Sulawesi.
Position & Duties

Main Duties and Functions

The Regional Office of the Directorate General of Immigration is tasked with executing specific duties of the Directorate General of Immigration at the provincial level. In its implementation, the Regional Office performs the following 5 main functions:

Operational Coordination
Operational coordination of all Technical Operating Units (UPT) within the South Sulawesi region.
Technical Guidance & Supervision
Technical immigration guidance and supervision of all UPT offices under its coordination.
Control & Evaluation
Control and evaluation of immigration task implementation, conducted periodically, measurably, and accountably.
Program & Budget Formulation
Formulating immigration work programs and budgets aligned with national policies and regional needs.
HR & Administrative Management
Managing human resources, personnel administration, and immigration facilities and infrastructure in the region.
Legal Basis
Regulation of the Minister of Immigration and Correctional Affairs No. 2 of 2024 on the Organization and Work Procedures of Regional Offices.

Public Services

Immigration Service Types

The South Sulawesi Regional Office through its UPTs provides various immigration services for both Indonesian citizens and foreign nationals.

Passport
An official document issued to Indonesian citizens as identification for international travel. Issued through data verification and administrative procedures.
Visa
Entry permit for foreign nationals into Indonesia. Includes visit visas, limited stay visas, and special-purpose visas issued based on purpose and duration of stay.
Stay Permit
Legal authorization for foreign nationals to reside in Indonesia. Includes visit stay permit, limited stay permit (ITAS/e-KITAS), and permanent stay permit (ITAP).
Other Services
Immigration checks at airports and seaports, monitoring of foreign nationals, administrative actions including deportation, and ongoing development of digital-based services.
Reference

Immigration Terms

Key immigration terms the public should know when dealing with immigration services.

TPI — Immigration Checkpoint
Immigration offices that provide direct entry and exit check services, similar to border crossing points like airports, seaports, and land borders.
Non-TPI
Immigration offices that do not provide direct entry and exit inspection services.
e-KITAS
Electronic Limited Stay Permit Card for foreign nationals residing in Indonesia.
Overstay
Staying beyond the permitted duration of stay granted by immigration authorities.
Entry Ban / Exit Ban
A prohibition from entering or leaving a country for a certain period due to deportation, expulsion, or serious immigration violations.
Detainee
A foreign national placed in an Immigration Detention House based on a detention decision issued by an Immigration Officer.
Entry / Exit Stamp
A stamp placed on a passport indicating the date and place of entry into or exit from a country.
Deportation
A forced removal of a foreign national from the territory of Indonesia in accordance with immigration laws.
Border Crossing Point
Official entry and exit points (airports, seaports, land borders) where immigration inspections are conducted.
